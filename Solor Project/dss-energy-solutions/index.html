About DSS Energy Solutions

Welcome to DSS Energy Solutions, Rajasthan's trusted partner in clean, green, and cost-effective energy solutions.

Founded on 1st December 2021 by Dilip Singh Sisodiya and Manisha Singh Sisodiya, our journey began with a powerful idea: to make natural energy sources accessible, beneficial, and rewarding for everyone.

As DSS Energy Private Limited, we are dedicated to helping residential, commercial, and industrial customers reduce their electricity expenses through reliable solar energy solutions.

As a government-registered and authorised solar vendor, we provide end-to-end solar services, including project planning, engineering, installation, premium-quality material supply, and government subsidy assistance (including PM Surya Ghar and state solar subsidies) alongside DISCOM net-metering approvals.
